Essential Reading

Textbook
Active Portfolio Management
Grinold & Kahn. The foundational text for systematic factor-based investing. Essential for understanding alpha, information ratio, and the fundamental law of active management.
 Textbook
Advances in Active Portfolio Management
Grinold & Kahn (sequel). More recent treatment of factor models, risk models, and portfolio construction. Extends the original work with modern applications.
 Textbook
Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives
John C. Hull. Standard reference for derivatives pricing, hedging, and risk management. Every QR member should know chapters on futures pricing and Greeks.
 Book
Systematic Trading
Robert Carver. Practical guide to building and managing systematic trading strategies. Directly applicable to our trend-following and portfolio construction work.
 Book
A Man for All Markets
Ed Thorp. Pioneer of quantitative finance. Essential perspective on risk management, expected value thinking, and the edge-seeking mindset.
 Paper
Time Series Momentum (Moskowitz et al.)
Foundational academic paper behind trend-following strategies. Essential reading for understanding the economic rationale behind our Trend Following IP.
