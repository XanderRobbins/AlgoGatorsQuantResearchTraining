Week 01 — Guided Onboarding

Core Infrastructure
Setup

Meet the team and bring the core stack online

Complete the administrative requirements, join AlgoGators systems, and get the trade and data engines running locally with hands-on team support.

Week 1 Goal Access first, then local infrastructure This is the first half of setup. Bring blockers to the team instead of spending the week stuck alone.
Step 1

Attend Xander’s Introduction to Finance Lectures

1 hour · Weeks 1–4

Attendance is required at Xander’s Introduction to Finance lecture during each of the first four weeks. QD analysts attend for financial context, but they do not complete the course homework.

  • Attend all four lectures and bring questions that connect the finance concepts to AlgoGators systems.
  • Do not complete or submit homework from the Introduction to Finance course.
  • Complete only the QD assignments: data-ngin work in Week 2 and trade-ngin work in Week 3.
Step 2

Administrative Setup

  • Review and sign the Participation Agreement.
  • Format your resume using the AlgoGators template.
  • Update LinkedIn with your AlgoGators role.
  • Submit your class schedule to your Team Lead and President.
  • Review the fund handbook and calendar.
Step 3

Join the Core Infrastructure

  • GitHub: join the AlgoGators organization, enable two-factor authentication, and configure Git on your computer.
  • Jira: accept the invitation, complete your profile, and confirm that you can open the team board. Week 4 is when you begin working from tickets.
  • Team channels: join WhatsApp and Discord, confirm OneDrive access, and meet personally with John Riley.
  • Trade engine: clone trade-ngin, follow its README, build it locally, and run its documented verification command.
  • Data engine: clone data-ngin, install its dependencies, and run its local tests from a clean checkout.

In-person setup support

After one of the team meetings, AlgoGators members will work with new analysts in person to resolve installation issues, permissions, compiler problems, and environment-specific blockers.

Week 1 completion standard: Your administrative tasks are complete, GitHub and Jira access work, and both trade-ngin and data-ngin can run a documented build, test, or startup command on your computer. Record unresolved blockers for Week 2.
