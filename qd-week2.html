Week 02 — Guided Onboarding

Database Setup &
Data Deliverable

Finish setup, connect to data, then use the pipeline

Complete the second half of the technical setup, connect through pgAdmin, and learn the data pipeline by completing a focused self-directed deliverable.

Week 2 Goal Finish the tedious setup work Setup is intentionally split across two weeks so analysts have time to solve machine-specific issues without rushing.
Step 1

Attend Xander’s Introduction to Finance Lecture

1 hour · Week 2 of 4

Attendance is required for the second of Xander’s four Introduction to Finance lectures. QD analysts attend for financial context, but they do not complete the course homework.

  • Attend the full lecture and take notes on concepts relevant to AlgoGators systems.
  • Do not complete or submit homework from the Introduction to Finance course.
  • Complete this week’s QD assignment: the separately provided data-ngin Data Deliverable.
Step 2

Database & Remaining Setup

  • Install and open pgAdmin.
  • Add the team PostgreSQL server using credentials supplied through the approved internal channel.
  • Confirm that you can connect, view the assigned database, and inspect the expected schemas.
  • Finish any remaining development tools or dependencies left from Week 1.
  • Confirm that data-ngin can reach the local or approved team database configuration.
Step 3

Complete the Data Deliverable

1–2 hours

The deliverable is the lesson: it is designed to teach the pipeline through direct use, so no additional in-person lecture is required. Assignment instructions and submission details are provided separately.

  • Work through the supplied Data Deliverable from a functioning local environment.
  • Use the pipeline and database tools instead of recreating the process manually.
  • Submit the requested output and note any unclear behavior or blocker.

Setup help remains available

Use the in-person period after a team meeting for pgAdmin, PostgreSQL, dependency, and permissions issues. Bring the exact error and the command that produced it.

Week 2 completion standard: pgAdmin opens and connects successfully, the remaining local setup blockers are resolved or assigned, and the Data Deliverable has been submitted.
