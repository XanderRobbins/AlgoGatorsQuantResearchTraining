Week 3 Goal Use the systems you configured The environment should now support practical work. This week tests whether you can use it to complete a defined systems task.
Step 1

Attend Xander’s Introduction to Finance Lecture

1 hour · Week 3 of 4

Attendance is required for the third of Xander’s four Introduction to Finance lectures. QD analysts attend for financial context, but they do not complete the course homework.

  • Attend the full lecture and take notes on concepts relevant to AlgoGators systems.
  • Do not complete or submit homework from the Introduction to Finance course.
  • Complete this week’s QD assignment: the separately provided trade-ngin Systems Deliverable.
Step 2

Complete the Systems Deliverable

Detailed assignment instructions are provided separately. Use those instructions as the source of truth for the task, required output, and submission format.

  • Start from a functioning local environment and the specified repository state.
  • Follow the supplied requirements and verify the result locally.
  • Document the commands used, the result produced, and any unresolved blocker.
  • Submit the deliverable through the specified team channel.
Week 3 completion standard: The assigned Systems Deliverable has been submitted, the result can be reproduced from the documented steps, and any blocker is specific enough for another team member to investigate.
