Key Messaging Pillars

Systematic Approach

All strategies are rules-based, fully transparent, and built on sound economic rationale. No discretionary black boxes — every signal can be explained and backtested.

Student-Led, Institutionally Rigorous

One of the only student-led systematic hedge funds in the country. Our research process mirrors institutional standards: out-of-sample testing, full documentation, leadership review.

Educational Mission

Non-profit structure. Our activities develop the next generation of quant finance professionals. Subscribers support student development and receive research-grade signals in return.

Open-Source Commitment

All tools and infrastructure are open-source under GPL v3. We contribute to the quant finance community, not just extract from it. Transparency is a competitive advantage.

Outreach Materials Checklist

  • Investor Pitch Deck — mission, team, strategies, and value proposition
  • One-Page Fact Sheet — performance snapshots, strategy summaries, key metrics
  • Strategy Whitepapers — detailed research behind each signal product
  • Quarterly Performance Report — live strategy performance, new IP updates, fund news
  • Alumni Newsletter — fund updates, member achievements, recruiting pipeline news
