2025–2030

Mission · Vision · KPI Milestones

AlgoGators is building a lasting institution. This is what we're working toward.

Mission & Vision

Mission

"...to democratize quantitative finance through a student-led hedge fund that facilitates experience and interdisciplinary collaboration among college students."

Vision

"...to establish a lasting legacy as the premier quantitative fund at the university level."

AlgoGators is one of the only student-led systematic hedge funds in the country. The 5-year plan is to build an institution that outlasts its founders — a self-sustaining research organization that trains quant finance professionals, produces market-relevant research, and maintains a track record that speaks for itself.

Strategic Pillars

Research Excellence
Rigorous IP process, high minimum standards, peer review, and honest reporting. Every strategy we deploy earns its place through evidence — not enthusiasm.
Technology Infrastructure
trade-ngin, data-ngin, and AlgoLens form an institutional-grade research and execution stack. Scale AWS infrastructure as strategy count grows. All open-source under GPL v3.
Talent Development
The 13-week analyst program, IP process, and quant prep resources are designed to make members genuinely competitive for top-tier internships and FT roles at quant firms.
External Recognition
Academic publications, industry partnerships, alumni network, and media presence that establish AlgoGators as a thought leader — not just a student club.

What This Means for You

You are part of something that has never existed before at this scale: a student-run systematic fund with institutional rigor, open-source tools, and a genuine track record in live markets. The work you do here — the IPs you build, the strategies you deploy, the code you ship — is real. It goes on AlgoLens. It goes in production. It gets scrutinized by the market.

The plan isn't to be the best student fund. The plan is to be the best fund, qualified. Do the work accordingly.
