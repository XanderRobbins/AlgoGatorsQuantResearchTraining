Week 4 Goal Transition from training into contribution You already have Jira access from Week 1. This week is about using tickets to plan, communicate, and begin real work.
Step 1

Attend Xander’s Final Introduction to Finance Lecture

1 hour · Week 4 of 4

Attendance is required for the final Introduction to Finance lecture. QD analysts attend for financial context, but they do not complete the course homework.

  • Attend the full lecture and bring any remaining finance questions.
  • Do not complete or submit homework from the Introduction to Finance course.
  • Review the Week 2 and Week 3 QD assignments and clarify any remaining training questions.
Step 2

End Of Training

  • Review the team’s Jira statuses, priorities, and definition of done.
  • Select your first ticket with your Team Lead rather than self-assigning unclear work.
  • Read the acceptance criteria, dependencies, linked documentation, and repository context.
  • Break down the first step and begin work from the correct branch or repository state.
  • Post progress and blockers directly on the ticket so the team has a shared record.
Week 4 completion standard: Guided onboarding is complete, you understand the Jira workflow, and your first assigned ticket is in progress with a clear next action and visible status.
