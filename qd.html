How QD Onboarding Works

Quantitative Development builds and runs the infrastructure the rest of the fund depends on. Before you write a line of production code, you learn what that infrastructure serves — so onboarding runs in two tracks: four weeks embedded with Quantitative Research under Xander, then two focused weeks on systems with Jonah and John — one fully dedicated to the data pipeline, the other fully dedicated to TradeNgin.

Weeks 1–4

Research Immersion

Embed with the QR desk under Xander. Learn the research workflow you will be building tools for.

Mentor

Xander — QR Lead

Your guide through Phase 1. You attend the same foundation sessions the QR analysts take.

Weeks 5–6

Systems Weeks

Two dedicated weeks — the data systems pipeline (W5), then TradeNgin, the firm's trade engine (W6).

Mentors

Jonah & John

Your guides through Phase 2. They own the pipeline and execution stack you will join.

Three-Phase Onboarding

Six weeks, three phases — building from research context to the two systems you will own.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4

Quantitative Research Immersion

Mentor: Xander — QR Lead

Embedded with the QR desk

For your first four weeks you work directly under Xander, the QR Lead, attending the same foundation sessions as the research analysts. You cannot build good infrastructure for research you do not understand — this is where you learn it.

  • What defines a research edge & the Investment Proposal workflow
  • Derivatives & contract mechanics
  • FX & commodity markets
  • Fixed income & equity factors
Phase 2 · Week 5

Data Systems Pipeline

Mentors: Jonah & John

A week dedicated to data

A full week on the pipeline that feeds every strategy. Jonah and John walk you through how raw market and reference data become clean, query-ready inputs for research and execution.

  • Data sourcing & ingestion
  • Cleaning, validation & storage
  • Pipeline → research → backtest data flow
  • Monitoring & data-quality gates
Phase 3 · Week 6

TradeNgin — The Trade Engine

Mentors: Jonah & John

A week dedicated to execution

A full week on TradeNgin, the engine that turns signals into live orders. You follow a signal end to end — from the pipeline's output all the way to a placed order.

  • TradeNgin architecture & components
  • Signal-to-order execution path
  • Order routing, fills & market impact
  • Risk checks & live-system safeguards
