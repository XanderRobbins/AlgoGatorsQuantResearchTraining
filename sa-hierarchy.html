Role Descriptions

President

Strategic oversight and external representation. Coordinates across all teams. Final decision-maker on fund direction and strategy approvals.

Vice President

Assists the President with micro-level management — oversees IPs, Capstones, and Deliverables. Acts as President in their absence.

Team Leads (QR / QT / QD / IR)

Manage and coordinate their respective teams. Provide mentorship, ensure team deliverables are met, and report to leadership weekly.

Analysts

Lead focused projects within their team. Coordinate small groups of general members and report directly to Team Leads.
